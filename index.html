ELECTION ANALYTICS / VIRGINIA

Virginia shifted right.
Five northern localities accounted for half the margin change.

Kamala Harris carried Virginia in 2024, but by a substantially smaller margin than Joe Biden four years earlier. The Democratic-minus-Republican margin narrowed in most localities—and some of the largest changes in raw vote margins occurred in populous Northern Virginia.

Virginia's Democratic margin fell from 451,138 votes in 2020 to 260,310 in 2024. That's a reduction of 190,828 votes even though the total number of presidential votes cast rose slightly.

That isn't simply a story about one big county. On a percentage-point basis, 120 of Virginia's 133 counties and independent cities shifted toward Republicans between the two elections. The shift was geographically broad, but populous places mattered most to the statewide vote difference.

Why geography and scale both matter

A small locality can experience a dramatic percentage-point shift and have little effect on the statewide total. A populous locality can shift by fewer points and still change the statewide vote margin by tens of thousands of votes.

For that reason, this report presents both measurements. It does not assume that any individual voter changed their preference. Changes in participation, the electorate's composition, third-party choices and persuasion could all affect aggregate results; locality totals alone cannot separate those mechanisms.

05 / TRANSPARENT METHODS

How the numbers were calculated

Data collection and checks

Data came from Virginia's official historical election database for the presidential contests in 2020 and 2024, downloaded October 28, 2026. Each file includes precinct records as well as locality totals. We used the 133 locality rows only, avoiding precinct double-counting. Locality totals reconcile exactly with the statewide results in both elections.

Definitions

D–R margin (percentage points) = 100 × (Democratic votes − Republican votes) ÷ votes recorded for president. R-ward margin shift = the 2020 D–R margin minus the 2024 D–R margin; it is not the change in Trump's vote share. Raw margin reduction = (Democratic − Republican votes in 2020) minus the corresponding value in 2024.

Interpretation limits

This compares geographic totals, not individual voter histories. It cannot determine who switched candidates or why. Votes recorded for president are not the same as all ballots cast or turnout among registered voters. The five Northern Virginia localities are a deliberately selected group, not an official regional boundary or the entire region.

THE BOTTOM LINE

What changed is clear. Why the totals shifted requires more evidence.

Virginia's 2024 presidential result was closer than in 2020. The data shows both a broad decline in Democratic-minus-Republican margins across localities and a disproportionate arithmetic contribution from several large Northern Virginia jurisdictions. Understanding the behavior behind those changes would require additional evidence—surveys, voter-file research and reporting on the ground.
